Questions

Post-Construction Cleaning questions.

How is my price calculated?

By your home size tier and how often we visit — not by the hour. You see the exact total before you confirm and it does not change unless you ask us to do more. First visits are priced as a deep clean to bring the home to a maintainable baseline.

Are you insured?

Our general liability policy is being put in place now, and it will be bound before we take our first job — ask us where it stands when you book and we will tell you straight. Every cleaner is an experienced independent contractor we vet personally and hold to a written service agreement before their first job.

How do I pay?

Payment is due on the day of service, and nothing is taken when you book. We'll confirm how you'd like to pay before the first visit. Commercial accounts are invoiced net-15.

What if something isn't right?

Tell us within 24 hours and we re-clean it free. That's our rescue protocol and there are no questions asked. Our owner also personally walks through your first and fourth visits.
