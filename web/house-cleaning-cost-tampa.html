The short answer

In the Tampa Bay area a recurring house cleaning typically runs $115 to $225 per visit, depending on the size of the home and how often you book. A one-time or first-time deep clean costs more, generally $240 to $385, because it covers built-up grime a maintenance visit does not reach.

A 2 BR / 2 BA home cleaned every two weeks is $150. The same home cleaned monthly is $180, because more time passes between visits. A 3 BR / 2 BA every two weeks is $185.

What actually changes the price

Size of the home

The single biggest factor. Our tiers run by bedrooms and square footage, from under 900 sq ft up to 3,000. Above 3,000 sq ft we quote individually rather than guess.

How often you book

Weekly is cheapest per visit because less builds up between cleans. Monthly costs more per visit for the same reason. A one-time clean is the dearest per visit of all.

Condition, not just size

First visits include a deep clean at +35% one time, to bring the home to a baseline the recurring price can maintain. After that you pay the standard rate.

Where you are

Tampa, St. Petersburg and Clearwater carry no travel charge. Outlying areas add a drive-time charge between $15 and $35, shown before you book rather than added afterwards.

Hourly or flat rate?

Plenty of Tampa cleaners quote by the hour, usually $25 to $50 an hour per cleaner. The trouble with an hourly rate is that you cannot know the bill until the work is finished, and a slower clean costs you more rather than less.

We price per visit instead. You see the number before you book, it does not move once quoted, and if something gets missed we come back and redo it rather than charging for another hour.

Common questions about cost

How much is a one-time house cleaning in Tampa?

A one-time clean runs $240 to $385 depending on home size, because a single visit has to deal with everything at once rather than maintaining a baseline.

Why does the first clean cost more?

The first visit is a deep clean, charged at +35% once. It brings the home to a standard the recurring price can hold. Every visit after that is the standard rate.

Is it cheaper to book weekly or monthly?

Weekly costs the least per visit and monthly the most, because the longer the gap between cleans the more there is to do. Monthly still costs less per month overall.

Do you charge extra to travel to my area?

Tampa, St. Petersburg and Clearwater have no travel charge. Outlying areas add $15 to $35 for drive time, and it is shown in your quote before you book, never added after.

Do I have to give a card to see a price?

No. The booking form prices your home with no card and no email address. You only enter details if you decide to book.
