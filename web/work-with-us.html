What this is, in plain terms

This is 1099 independent contractor work, not a job. That cuts both ways and you should know which parts cut which way before you apply.

What you get

You pick the jobs you want. Nobody schedules you against your will. You are paid per job at a rate you agreed in advance, and a job that takes you less time pays the same. Tips are yours.

What you carry

Your own transport, your own supplies and equipment, and your own general liability insurance. You handle your own taxes — nothing is withheld. There are no guaranteed hours, no holiday pay and no benefits.

If you want fixed hours and a payslip, this is honestly the wrong arrangement and we would rather tell you now than in week two.

What we expect

Turn up when you said

The single thing that loses customers. If something goes wrong, tell us early rather than quietly.

Work to the checklist

Every job has a written list. It is how "clean" stops being a matter of opinion, and it protects you as much as the customer.

Photos at the end

Before and after, uploaded when you finish. It is the evidence the work was done properly.

Say if you cannot

Nobody is penalised for turning a job down. People are dropped for accepting and not appearing.

Questions

How much work is there?

We are a new company and honest about it: there is not a full week of work waiting for you today. It suits someone building up their own client base or filling gaps around existing work, and it grows as we do.

Do I need my own insurance?

Yes, general liability. It typically runs $30-60 a month for a solo cleaner. We cannot send you to a customer's home without it.

Do I need my own supplies?

Yes - supplies, equipment and transport are yours. That is part of why the split is 50% rather than an hourly wage.

When am I paid?

Weekly, every Friday, covering the jobs you completed the previous Monday to Sunday, after the checklist is signed off.

Do I have to take every job offered?

No. Jobs are offered and you accept or decline. What matters is that once you accept, you turn up.
