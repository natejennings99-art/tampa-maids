Questions

Everything people ask us.

If your question isn't here, call us or send a message — a real person replies.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Most of our clients give us a door code, lockbox or key, all logged in a chain-of-custody record. You'll get a text when the crew arrives, and before/after photos in your account when they finish.

How is my price calculated?

By your home size tier and how often we visit — not by the hour. You see the exact total before you confirm and it does not change unless you ask us to do more. First visits are priced as a deep clean to bring the home to a maintainable baseline.

Why is the first clean more expensive?

A first visit is priced as a deep clean, about 35% above your recurring rate. It covers the built-up work a maintenance visit doesn't reach — baseboards, grout, inside appliances, vents. Every visit after that is at your normal recurring price.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, at no extra charge. HEPA backpack vacuums, a color-coded microfiber system laundered between every job, and EPA Safer Choice certified concentrates. If you'd rather we use your products, leave them out and note it on your booking.

Are you insured?

Our general liability policy is being put in place now, and it will be bound before we take our first job — ask us where it stands when you book and we will tell you straight. Every cleaner is an experienced independent contractor we vet personally and hold to a written service agreement before their first job.

Will I get the same cleaners each visit?

For recurring clients, yes. We assign a dedicated two-person team so they learn your home and your preferences. If someone is out, we brief the replacement with your notes and photos first.

What if something isn't right?

Tell us within 24 hours and we re-clean it free. That's our rescue protocol and there are no questions asked. Our owner also personally walks through your first and fourth visits.

What's your cancellation policy?

Residential service is month-to-month. Reschedule or cancel free up to 48 hours before your visit from the app or by phone. Inside 48 hours a fee applies because the crew's time is already reserved.

How do I pay?

Residential clients are charged to a saved card on the day of service. Commercial accounts are invoiced net-15. We accept all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Do you handle vacation rental turnovers?

It's one of our specialties. We run a 28-point turnover checklist with full linen re-set, consumable restocking, photo verification and a same-day SLA inside your check-out/check-in window, seven days a week in peak season.

Do you charge sales tax?

Cleaning of residential dwellings is exempt from Florida sales tax under F.S. 212.05, so your home cleaning has no tax added. Commercial and non-dwelling cleaning is taxable and shows the tax line on your invoice.

What areas do you serve?

We cover four markets: Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater and Sarasota, along with the communities around each — South Tampa, Westchase, Brandon, Riverview, Snell Isle, Pinellas Park, Gulfport, St. Pete Beach, Treasure Island, Largo, Seminole, Dunedin, Safety Harbor, Siesta Key, Longboat Key and Lakewood Ranch. We're based in Tampa. A small drive-time surcharge applies to the farthest beach, eastern and Sarasota addresses. If you're just outside, call us — we can often still make it work.

What happens during hurricane season?

If a storm closes the area we contact you to reschedule at no charge, and we prioritize getting your recurring slot back first. We also offer post-storm cleanup as a separate service.
